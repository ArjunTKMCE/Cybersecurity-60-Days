# Cybersecurity Basics

## CIA Triad
- **Confidentiality:** Prevent unauthorized access to information.
- **Integrity:** Keep information accurate and protected from unauthorized changes.
- **Availability:** Keep systems and data accessible when needed.

## Common Threats
- **Phishing:** Deceptive messages or websites used to trick users.
- **Malware:** Malicious software such as viruses, worms, trojans, spyware, and ransomware.
- **DDoS:** Attempts to make a service unavailable by overwhelming it with traffic or requests.
- **SQL Injection:** Improperly handled input can alter SQL queries; parameterized queries help prevent it.
- **Brute Force:** Repeatedly trying credentials until a valid combination is found.
- **Ransomware:** Malware that can deny access to data, commonly through encryption, and demand payment.
- **Social Engineering:** Manipulating people into taking unsafe actions or revealing information.
- **Wireless Attacks:** Attacks involving weaknesses in Wi-Fi authentication, encryption, configuration, or rogue access points.
- **Insider Threats:** Security risks involving authorized users, intentionally or accidentally.

## Basic Defenses
Strong passwords, MFA, patching, backups, least privilege, monitoring, secure configuration, and security awareness.
